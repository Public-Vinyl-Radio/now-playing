# Bundled assets

## Sample album artwork

`artwork/con-todo-el-mundo.jpg` is the cover of Khruangbin's *Con Todo El Mundo*, sourced from the artist's [Bandcamp album page](https://khruangbin.bandcamp.com/album/con-todo-el-mundo-excluding-n-s-america), via that page's Open Graph artwork URL: `https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a4178091556_5.jpg`.

The artwork belongs to its rights holders. It is included as sample artwork for this private listening room prototype; no open source license is claimed for the cover. Replace it with artwork supplied by the listening room's media entity in Phase 2.

## Station logo

`branding/pvr-logo.svg` is the user-provided PVR logo from `PVR-Logo-Landcape-short.svg`. Its viewBox is cropped to the visible mark and hidden export layers are removed. The scene tints its white areas warm ivory and includes the mark in the CRT rendering pipeline.

## App icons

The PVR icons are original SVG text and line graphics in `icon.svg`, with PNG exports for Home Screen use.
